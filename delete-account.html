Delete Your Account

How to request account deletion in the Spine app

You can delete your Spine account directly from within the app at any time. Follow the steps below to permanently delete your account and associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Spine app on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap the Settings icon in the top right corner.
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  1. Your profile and username
  2. Your entire movie collection
  3. Your wishlist
  4. Your activity history
  5. All followers and following relationships

Data Retention

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All data is removed immediately upon confirmation. No personal data is retained after deletion.

Need Help?

If you have trouble deleting your account or would like to request deletion by email, contact us at support@getspineapp.com.
